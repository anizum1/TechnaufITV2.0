Healthcare · North Dallas

A 40-person medical practice moved to the cloud over one weekend

ClientNorth Dallas Family Medicine
IndustryHealthcare
Size40 staff · 3 locations
Cloud MigrationHIPAA readinessCloud Backup24/7 Monitoring

The challenge

An aging on-premise server was failing weekly, and their previous provider took up to two days to answer a ticket. Patient records sat on hardware with no tested backup, and an upcoming HIPAA audit had leadership genuinely worried.

What we did

  1. Full environment and HIPAA gap assessment in the first two weeks, delivered as a plain-English report.
  2. Designed a cloud environment with encryption at rest and in transit, MFA on every account, and role-based access mapped to job function.
  3. Migrated records, imaging, and email across a single weekend window, with a tested rollback plan in reserve.
  4. Stood up immutable backups with quarterly restore testing, and enrolled the practice in 24/7 monitoring.

Results

0minutes of unplanned downtime since cutover
11minute average first response, including weekends
100%of HIPAA technical safeguards evidenced for audit

Our old provider took two days to answer a ticket. Technauf answered in eleven minutes on a Sunday. They migrated our entire practice to the cloud over one weekend, and we haven't had a minute of unplanned downtime since.

Legal · Fort Worth

The security assessment three vendors missed — and the phishing attempt it stopped

ClientThornton & Cole LLP
IndustryLegal
Size65 staff · 1 office
Security AssessmentEndpoint ProtectionIAMAwareness Training

The challenge

The firm had been through three IT vendors in five years and assumed it was well protected. Privileged client matter files were accessible far more broadly than partners realised, and MFA was enabled for some staff but not for administrators.

What we did

  1. Ran a full-stack security assessment, including authenticated internal scanning and an access review by department.
  2. Found and closed over-broad file permissions, then rebuilt access on a least-privilege model matched to practice groups.
  3. Enforced MFA on every account — including the service and administrator accounts previous vendors had exempted.
  4. Rolled out EDR with automatic device isolation, plus monthly phishing simulations and short training modules.

Results

1phishing attempt contained before any file was touched
3previous vendors' blind spots closed
0accounts left without multi-factor authentication

The security assessment found gaps three previous vendors missed. Six months later, their monitoring caught and contained a phishing attempt before a single file was touched. That's the difference between a vendor and a partner.

JTJames ThorntonManaging Partner · Thornton & Cole LLP, Fort Worth
Logistics · Arlington

Three warehouses, constant scanner dropouts, and a network rebuilt from the floor up

ClientLoneStar Logistics
IndustryLogistics & Warehousing
Size180 staff · 3 warehouses
Wi-Fi OptimizationNetwork DesignSD-WANNetwork Troubleshooting

The challenge

Handheld scanners dropped mid-scan across all three warehouses, forcing staff to re-key shipments by hand. Two previous providers had added more access points, which made interference worse rather than better.

What we did

  1. Ran on-site wireless surveys in all three warehouses under real operating conditions — racking full, forklifts moving.
  2. Found the root cause was co-channel interference and AP placement, not insufficient coverage, and redesigned the layout accordingly.
  3. Rebuilt the wireless network with proper channel planning, power tuning, and separate networks for scanners, staff, and guests.
  4. Linked all three sites with SD-WAN so a circuit failure at one warehouse no longer stalls the others.

Results

90%reduction in scanner errors
3sites linked with automatic circuit failover
0manual re-keying shifts since the rebuild

We run three warehouses across DFW and the Wi-Fi used to drop mid-scan constantly. Technauf redesigned the whole network and rolled out SD-WAN. Scanner errors are down 90% and our ops team finally trusts the system.
