We're a Plano-based team of cloud, network, and security engineers looking after real environments for real companies. No ticket mills, no scripts to read from — you'll own problems end to end and see the outcome.

Why Technauf

What you get here

Certifications on us

We cover exam fees and study time for the certs that matter in this work — CISSP, CCNP, AWS and Azure security tracks, CompTIA. Renewals included, not just the first attempt.

Real mentorship

Every engineer is paired with a senior on their track. Juniors sit in on architecture reviews and incident calls from week one — you learn by being in the room, not by waiting your turn.

Varied environments

Healthcare, legal, manufacturing, finance — you'll touch far more stacks and constraints in a year here than in a decade on a single internal IT team.

Sane on-call

After-hours rotation is shared, paid, and predictable. We staff for coverage instead of burning out the person who happens to answer.
