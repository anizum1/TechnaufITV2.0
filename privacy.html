Privacy Policy
Plainly stated.

We collect the minimum we need to answer your enquiry, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Who we are

Technauf provides cloud, cybersecurity, network, and managed IT services from Plano, Texas, to businesses across the United States. For any privacy question, contact us through the contact form.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. Through this website that means:

  • Contact and consultation forms — your name, company, email address, the service you selected, and the message you write.
  • Checklist and resource downloads — your email address and, if you provide it, your company name.
  • Technical information — standard web server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, generated automatically when any website is visited.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, sensitive personal information, protected health information, or payment card details through these forms.

How we use it

We use what you submit to reply to your enquiry, provide the resource you requested, and — if you downloaded a resource — send occasional related IT guidance. Every such email includes an unsubscribe link.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Form processing

Website form submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers them to our email. Your submission passes through and is stored on Formspree's systems in accordance with their own privacy policy. We chose a processor rather than storing submissions on this site so there is no database of enquiries sitting on a web server.

Analytics

This site is built to run with no third-party analytics at all. If website analytics are enabled in future, they will be configured to anonymise IP addresses and to respect browser Do Not Track signals, and this policy will be updated to name the provider before that happens.

Embedded content

The contact page embeds a Google Map, and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser loads those, it makes a request to Google's servers, which will see your IP address and is governed by Google's privacy policy. No other third-party content is embedded.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Small preferences — such as whether you have muted interface sounds — are stored locally in your own browser and are never transmitted to us.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are retained for as long as needed to serve you and to keep ordinary business records, and are reviewed periodically. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are legally required to retain it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us, and you may unsubscribe from any email at any time. Send the request through our contact form and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Texas residents and residents of jurisdictions with comparable privacy laws may have additional rights; we will honour valid requests regardless of where you live.

Security

We apply the same practices we recommend to clients: least-privilege access to enquiry data, multi-factor authentication on the accounts that can read it, and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not ask you to accept protections we would not deploy ourselves.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

A note on scope

This policy covers the technauf.com website. Personal or client data we process while delivering managed services is governed by the written agreement with that client, including any Business Associate Agreement where protected health information is involved.
